Artisan & Sourdough Bakehouses

Designed for bakeries prioritizing long-fermentation doughs, open irregular crumb structures, and dark caramelized stone hearth crusts.

Recommended Equipment Package:
  • Ovens: Vulcano 3-Deck or 4-Deck Modular Stone Hearth Ovens with Independent Steam

Turnkey Execution

The 4-Stage Commissioning Process

How our sales and mechanical engineering teams transition your bakery from concept blueprints to active daily production.

Step 01
CAD Layout & Utilities

We verify gas supply pipes (BTU/hr), 3-phase electrical feeds, floor load capacities, and exhaust hood CFM clearances.

Step 02
Freight & Millwright Rigging

Heavy machinery delivery, doorway clearance coordination, precision leveling, and bolting into permanent positions.

Step 03
Factory Burn-In & Test

Mechanical and combustion testing, steam generator pressure verification, and multi-hour thermal stabilization burns.

Step 04
Staff Operation Training

Master baker instruction for your head bakers on digital recipe programming, steam timing, and daily maintenance routines.
