Commercial Privacy Policy

How BakeTech Commercial Systems LLC protects proprietary commercial equipment inquiries, billing records, and technical consultation data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Reviewed: Q4 2026

1. Scope of This Commercial Policy

BakeTech Commercial Systems LLC ("BakeTech", "we", "us", or "our") acts as an authorized equipment distributor, engineering advisor, and service provider to commercial bakeries, institutional kitchens, hotels, and food manufacturers. This policy describes how we collect, store, utilize, and protect business information submitted through our customer-facing commercial portal, quote request forms, and showroom consultations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect corporate and commercial contact details necessary to process equipment quotations, arrange logistics, and schedule certified rigging:

  • Commercial Identification: Bakery name, business registration, primary contact name, title, and facility address.
  • Technical Utility Specifications: Electrical drop capacity (208V, 240V, 480V 3-Phase), gas line availability (BTU/hr, gas pressure), and doorway/loading dock clearances.
  • Financial & Credit Data: Equipment leasing applications, tax-exempt resale certificates, and Section 179 qualification records processed through licensed third-party underwriting partners.
  • Telemetry & Showroom Demos: Recipe trial parameters, target bread crust profiles, and equipment performance logs generated during showroom baking trials.

3. How Information Is Utilized

Corporate data submitted to BakeTech is strictly utilized for commercial purposes: generating itemized machine price quotations, freight routing through bonded carriers, processing OEM warranty registrations with European and North American manufacturers, and issuing technical service bulletins. We do not sell, rent, or lease your commercial data to third-party marketing firms.

4. Data Security & Storage Standards

All electronic communications, quotation forms, and financing inquiries are transmitted via 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption. Internal databases are maintained in secure SOC-2 certified cloud infrastructure with strict multi-factor role-based access controls. Hard-copy engineering schematics and facility CAD blueprints are kept under secure commercial custody.

5. Cookies & Site Analytics

Our customer-facing portal uses essential session cookies to remember theme preferences (Dark / Light mode) and layout direction (LTR / RTL). We also deploy aggregated, non-personally identifiable analytics to track equipment category demand and optimize page load performance.

6. Commercial Contact & Opt-Out

If your bakery wishes to inspect, amend, or delete your technical contact profile from our dealership records, please contact our Compliance Office:
