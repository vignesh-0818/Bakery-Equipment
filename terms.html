Commercial Terms of Service

Equipment quotation validity, FOB freight logistics, rigging terms, warranty administration, and customer responsibilities.

Standard Commercial Terms • Revision 2026.1 Effective: January 2026

1. Commercial Agreement & Quotation Validity

All written machine proposals, itemized equipment quotes, and factory pricing schedules issued by BakeTech Commercial Systems LLC are valid for a period of thirty (30) calendar days from the date of issue unless specified otherwise in writing. Due to international maritime freight volatility and raw stainless steel indices, pricing for custom modular continuous tunnel lines may require price re-benchmarking prior to order signing.

2. Freight, Delivery & Title Transfer (FOB Points)

Unless otherwise specified in a turnkey purchase order, commercial equipment is shipped F.O.B. BakeTech Logistics Depot (Chicago, IL) or F.O.B. Origin manufacturer facility. Risk of loss passes to the purchaser upon carrier sign-off at the freight dock. Purchasers are required to conduct an immediate visual inspection of shipping crates and sign carrier bills of lading noting any outer crate damage prior to offloading.

3. Turnkey Rigging & Customer Utility Responsibilities

When Turnkey Rigging & Commissioning is contracted through BakeTech, the customer agrees to provide:

  • Doorway & Pathway Clearance: Adequate overhead and corridor clearance to transfer heavy modular deck ovens and spiral mixers to the final bakehouse pad.
  • Utility Connection Points: Dedicated electrical drops terminated within six (6) feet of the machinery footprint matching machine nameplate amperage (208V, 240V, or 480V 3-Phase).
  • Gas Supply & Regulators: Clean natural gas or LPG manifold supply calibrated to operating burner WC pressures as specified on pre-installation drawings.
  • Exhaust Ventilation: Certified Type-I commercial exhaust canopy operational prior to factory burner firing cycles.

4. Factory OEM Warranties & Preventive Care

All new machinery is covered by a minimum two (2) year factory OEM replacement parts warranty and one (1) year labor warranty on mechanical components. Consumables including silicone door gaskets, baking stones broken due to thermal shock from external cold liquid, and oven halogen lamps are excluded from standard defect warranties. All warranties require installation by licensed technicians in compliance with local sanitary and electrical codes.

5. Financing Terms & Section 179 Advisory

Equipment Finance Agreements (EFA), $1.00 Buyout Capital Leases, and Fair Market Value (FMV) leases are administered through independent federally licensed commercial equipment underwriting partners. Tax savings illustrations regarding Internal Revenue Code Section 179 are provided for general educational modeling only. Purchasers should consult their certified public accountant (CPA) regarding specific corporate depreciation deductions.

6. Returns, Cancellations & Restocking

Because commercial bakery equipment is configured to order (voltage, stone depth, steam manifold side), cancellations after production commencement are subject to a standard 20% restocking fee. Custom-manufactured conveyor lines, specialized dough stamping tooling, and continuous tunnel ovens are non-cancellable once structural fabrication has begun.

7. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any commercial arbitration or legal dispute arising under this agreement shall be conducted exclusively within Cook County, Illinois.
