Who We Are

The Trusted Dealer for Commercial Food Production

At BakeTech Commercial Systems, we bridge the gap between world-class European and North American bakery machine manufacturers and commercial bakery operators.

Founded by veteran oven technicians and master bakers, our organization understands that commercial bakery equipment is not a generic commodity. An oven must deliver consistent refractory stone thermal replenishment under continuous pan loading. A spiral mixer must knead high-hydration dough without friction heating. A dough sheeter must maintain uniform roller tolerances across thousands of laminated sheets.

Safety & Compliance

Certified Engineering & Sanitation Standards

Every commercial machine supplied by BakeTech is certified to North American and international electrical, sanitary, and combustion standards.

NSF / ANSI Standard 4 Sanitation

Commercial cooking, retarding, and processing equipment certified for food contact safety, seamless radiused internal corners, and effortless wash-down sanitization.

UL 197 & CSA C22.2 Commercial Electric

Multi-deck and convection electrical components rigorously certified for industrial voltage drops, motor overload protections, and dual-contact safety interlocks.

NFPA 54 & ANSI Z83.11 Gas Standards

Natural gas and LPG burners engineered with electronic flame safeguards, dual gas shut-off solenoids, and low NOx high-efficiency heat exchangers.

NFPA 96 Ventilation Integration

Dedicated engineering team designs Type-1 and Type-2 exhaust canopies, calculation of makeup air CFM, and condensation drain line routing.

ISO 9001:2015 Manufacturing Quality

Every deck oven, spiral mixer, and sheeter manufactured under audited quality control ensuring 100% component traceability and tight tolerances.

Pre-Delivery Burn-In Validation

Before dispatch, our Chicago engineering team runs an 8-hour continuous thermal stabilization and steam cycle test on every machine.
