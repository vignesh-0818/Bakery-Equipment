Why Commercial Bakeries Choose Equipment Financing

Instead of tying up credit lines or liquid cash in heavy machinery, our financing options allow you to retain working capital for payroll, flour inventory, marketing, and commercial lease reserves.

Programs Comparison

Choose Your Commercial Lease Structure

Compare the three most common commercial leasing agreements we structure for bakery owners.

Option 01

$1.00 Buyout Capital Lease

Treats the equipment as an owned asset from day one. At the conclusion of your 24 to 60-month term, you purchase the machinery for exactly $1.00.

  • Guaranteed full ownership at term end
  • Maximize Section 179 depreciation
  • Best for long-term stone deck ovens
Option 02

Fair Market Value (FMV) Lease

Offers the lowest possible monthly payment by incorporating residual value. Ideal for bakeries planning to upgrade to newer convection ovens or mixers every 3-4 years.

  • Lowest initial monthly obligation
  • Payments usually 100% tax deductible
  • Option to return, upgrade, or buy out
Option 03

Deferred & Seasonal Plans

Engineered for new bakery construction or seasonal resort bakehouses. Defer first monthly payments for up to 90 days or schedule lower payments during slow months.

  • Generate revenue before payments start
  • Smooths out commercial buildout cash crunch
  • Custom-tailored to holiday production peak

Maximize First-Year Cash Savings with Section 179 Tax Deductions

Commercial bakeries can deduct 100% of qualifying commercial ovens, spiral mixers, and dough lines in the first year of operation, effectively slashing equipment acquisition costs by your corporate tax rate.

By financing via an Equipment Finance Agreement or a $1 Buyout Lease, your business pays manageable monthly installments while writing off the entire machinery value immediately against taxable operating income.

Tax Incentive Strategy

Maximize First-Year Cash Savings with IRS Section 179

Internal Revenue Code Section 179 allows qualifying commercial bakeries to deduct 100% of the equipment purchase price from gross taxable income in the year placed into service.

By combining a $1 Buyout Capital Lease with Section 179, your bakery can take the full write-off immediately—even though your cash outflow is distributed across 36 to 60 affordable monthly installments.
