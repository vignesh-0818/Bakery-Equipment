Engineering Focus

The Anatomy of a Commercial Baking Oven

Commercial baking is thermodynamics in action. Our stone deck and rotary rack ovens feature proprietary heat storage alloys and pressurized steam injection for unmatched crust blister and caramelized crust.

Refractory Cordierite Stone Hearth

Delivers direct conductive heat transfer into dough bottoms, ensuring maximum vertical oven spring and blistered artisan crusts.

Independent High-Pressure Steam Generators

Individual steam boilers per deck produce instant saturated steam at the touch of a button, keeping starch pliable for maximum crumb expansion.

Microprocessor Recipe Automation

Store up to 99 multi-stage baking curves with motorized steam exhaust dampers, automatic temperature ramps, and end-of-bake alerts.
